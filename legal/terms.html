1. Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You must give a mobile number you actually control.
  • One person, one account. Do not sign in as someone else.

2. What you may list

Livestock, farm produce and inputs, farm machinery, and vehicles — where it is lawful for you to sell them.

You must not list:

  • anything you do not own or are not entitled to sell;
  • stolen animals or goods;
  • protected or endangered species, or anything whose sale, transport or slaughter is restricted by law;
  • sick animals described as healthy;
  • false prices, fake photos, or another person's photos;
  • anything offensive, or anyone's personal details without their permission.

You are responsible for complying with the laws of Tamil Nadu and of India on the keeping, transport, sale and slaughter of animals, including the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act and any state rules that apply to you.

3. Honest listings

  • Photos must be of the actual animal or item you are selling.
  • Describe condition, age and health truthfully.
  • Mark an item as sold once it is sold.

4. Contacting each other

Your phone number is private by default. If you turn on Call or WhatsApp contact, your number becomes visible to people viewing your listings. You can turn it off again at any time. Do not use another person's contact details for anything except the sale in question.

5. Moderation

Anyone can report a listing. We may remove a listing, or suspend an account, where we believe these terms have been broken — including after a report. A suspended account loses access and its listings are taken down. We aim to review reports promptly, but we do not promise to detect every problem.

  • We do not guarantee that the service is always available or error free.
  • We are not responsible for what you agree with another user, for the condition of anything you buy, or for money you choose to send.

Nothing here limits any right you have under Indian consumer law.

7. Ending your use

You may delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms.

The service is currently a trial. Sign-in does not yet verify that a mobile number belongs to the person using it. Treat listings and identities accordingly, and do not rely on the service for valuable transactions until verified sign-in is enabled.
