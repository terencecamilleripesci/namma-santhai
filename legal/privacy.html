What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not show your phone number to other users unless you turn on Call or WhatsApp contact yourself. It is off by default.
  • We strip the location and camera information stored inside your photos before publishing them, so a photo does not reveal where you live.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may:

  • See your data — Profile → Settings → Download my data.
  • Correct it — edit your profile and listings in the app.
  • Delete it — Profile → Settings → Delete my account. This removes your account, your listings, your photos and your messages from our servers. It cannot be undone.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off contact sharing or notifications at any time.
  • Complain — to our Grievance Officer above, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

4. Children

Namma Santhai is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that an account belongs to a child we will delete it.

5. Security

Your session is protected by a token on your device. Photos are re-processed on our servers, which removes embedded location data. We limit how often sensitive actions can be attempted.

Trial notice. This is currently a trial. Sign-in is by mobile number without an SMS code, which means it does not prove that the number belongs to you. Do not put anything sensitive in the app during the trial, and do not rely on it for valuable transactions until verified sign-in is enabled.

6. Where your data is held

Your data is stored on servers operated for Namma Santhai. We do not transfer personal data to any country that the Government of India has restricted.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.
